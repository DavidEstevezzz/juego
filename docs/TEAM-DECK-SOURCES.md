## Ormora generation prompt (built-in ImageGen)

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one wide 16:9 cinematic website hero image for a dark maritime horror game.
Input image 1: reference image for the identity, hull design and industrial deck architecture of the Ormora ship.
Primary request: recreate this same distinctive old industrial whaling ship in a more sinister, credible high-end game cinematic. Preserve the reference's long low black riveted hull, broad blunt bow, long wooden foredeck with its low rectangular deckhouse, dense central industrial superstructure, and tall cylindrical smokestack vocabulary. Show three smokestacks and preserve the reference's industrial deck geometry rather than redesigning it into a passenger vessel.
Scene/backdrop: ice-choked polar water, fractured ice plates in the foreground, distant snowy mountains nearly swallowed by ominous fog.
Composition/framing: wide landscape 16:9, lower three-quarter bow viewpoint, the full ship silhouette readable, main subject centered toward the right and lower half, ample quiet dark fog and sky negative space at upper left for website typography that will be added separately.
Lighting/mood: sinister and oppressive but beautifully readable, cold rim light revealing hull edges, layered volumetric fog behind the ship, restrained dying amber lights in a handful of portholes. Preserve shadow detail; do not crush the ship into black mush.
Color palette: desaturated cold teal, charcoal, steel gray, sparse muted amber.
Materials: weathered black riveted iron, frost, snow, damp wood, restrained salt and rust wear; detailed believable cinematic realism.
Constraints: one ship only, no text or lettering or logos or watermark, no fantasy tentacles, no monsters, no modern cruise liner, no extra fantasy towers, no bright saturated blue, no decorative border. Keep the ship's recognizable identity from the reference. Output only one polished image.
